About Wordly

A small, focused tool — built to do one thing well.

Why we built it

Most word counters online are cluttered with ads, slow to load, or ask you to sign in before you can type a single sentence. Wordly exists to be the opposite: a fast, clean, private tool that opens instantly and gets out of your way.

What it does

Wordly counts words, characters, sentences and paragraphs, and estimates reading time — all inside your browser, with no server round-trip. That means results appear instantly, and your text is never transmitted anywhere.

Who it's for

Students checking essays against a limit, writers tracking pacing, marketers fitting copy to a character count, and anyone who just wants to know how long their text is. If you write, you'll probably find a use for it.

How it's built

Wordly is built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript — no frameworks, no backend, no database. That keeps it fast and keeps your text local to your device. You can read more about exactly what that means for your privacy on our privacy page.
