Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

The short version

Text you type or paste into the word counter is processed entirely inside your browser using JavaScript. It is never uploaded to a server, transmitted over the network, or stored by us in any form. When you close or reload the page, that text is gone.

Text you enter into the tool

The counter reads the content of the editor directly in your browser to calculate words, characters, sentences, paragraphs, and reading time. This processing happens on your device. We have no server-side component that receives, logs, or retains what you write.

Site operation data

Like most websites, the server that delivers these files may automatically log basic technical information (such as IP address, browser type, and request timestamps) as part of standard web server operation. This is separate from — and unrelated to — the text you enter into the counter, which never reaches our servers.

Cookies and local storage

The core word-counting functionality does not require or set any cookies. We do not use cookies to track your text or your identity for the purpose of operating the tool.

Third-party advertising

This site may display advertising served by third parties, such as Google AdSense. Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads and measure their performance, and may collect information about your visits to this and other websites, in accordance with their own privacy policies. We do not control this data collection directly. If you'd like to opt out of personalized advertising, you can visit your ad settings through your browser or the relevant provider's opt-out tools.

Analytics

At the time of writing, this site does not run analytics software. If analytics are added in the future, this policy will be updated to describe what is collected and why, before or at the time such a change takes effect.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the site operator's contact details, where provided.
